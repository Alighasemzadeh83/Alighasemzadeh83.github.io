Ali Ghasemzadeh

I'm a final-year B.Sc. student in Computer Engineering at Sharif University of Technology (SUT), with a minor in Mathematics (GPA 18.83/20). Most of my research focuses on Computer Vision and Medical AI, where I am particularly interested in developing and understanding learning-based methods for visual and medical data. Alongside this work, I also explore theoretical questions in Estimation Theory.

Across my work I have led the idea development, implementation, and experimentation for several projects, and contributed to a number of surveys and research papers spanning medical vision, implicit neural representations, domain adaptation, and estimation theory. I completed an in-person research internship at The University of Hong Kong from Jul 2026 to Sep 2026.
