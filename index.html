BIO

I am a fourth-year Computer Engineering student at Sharif University of Technology, with a minor in Mathematics. My research spans optimization (convex, non-convex, and evolutionary methods), machine learning, and computer vision, including adversarial training, domain adaptation, and LLM-based reasoning. Supported by a strong mathematical background and extensive self-study, I have built solid foundations in optimization and learning theory.

I complement this grounding with substantial practical expertise: implementing and extending deep-learning and vision models in PyTorch, designing experiments, and planning ablations. Through contributing to two surveys and two research papers, I have gained experience in literature synthesis, paper writing, experimental methodology, and structuring large-scale reviews.

As the lead researcher within the team in several projects, I have organized the team, assigned tasks based on members' strengths, contributed to idea development, and carried out a major share of the implementation and experimentation across all stages of the work.
