Hi, I'm Ali — I work on computer vision, estimation theory & convex optimization.

I'm a final-year B.Sc. student in Computer Engineering at Sharif University of Technology (SUT), with a minor in Mathematics (GPA 18.83/20). I like problems that sit between rigorous theory and real systems — from the geometry of semidefinite & convex programs and estimation theory, to computer vision and medical AI.

Across my work I have led the idea development, implementation, and experimentation for several projects, and contributed to a number of surveys and research papers spanning medical vision, implicit neural representations, domain adaptation, and optimization. I'm currently a research intern at The University of Hong Kong.
